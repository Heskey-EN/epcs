# Topic backlog

Evergreen and scheduled ideas for days without a qualifying news story, plus the pillar guides that anchor the clusters in `PLAYBOOK.md` section 5. The daily run takes the top suitable item and deletes it from this list when it's published. The weekly review re-orders and refills the list (keep at least 15). Every item still needs fresh research and sources on the day — the notes below are prompts, not verified facts.
